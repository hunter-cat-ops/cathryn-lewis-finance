Cathryn Lewis — Financial Analysis Toolkit

Turn a price history into risk and return numbers.

This site calculates how a stock has performed, how risky it has been, and how it moves with the market. Paste in prices, pick a benchmark, and get results in seconds.

Performance

Holding-period return, annualized return, excess return over a benchmark, Sharpe and Treynor ratios.

Risk

Volatility, beta, CAPM alpha, and correlation between two stocks.

Valuation

Market cap, P/E ratio, dividend yield, and book-to-market ratio.

Methods used

  • Simple periodic returns: Pt / Pt-1 − 1
  • Annualized return: (1 + holding-period return)periods per year / number of periods − 1
  • Volatility: sample standard deviation of periodic returns, multiplied by √(periods per year)
  • Beta: Cov(excess stock return, excess market return) / Var(excess market return)
  • CAPM alpha: average excess stock return − beta × average excess market return, annualized
  • Sharpe: annualized excess return / annualized volatility. Treynor: annualized excess return / beta
